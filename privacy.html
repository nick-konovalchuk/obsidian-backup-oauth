ObsidianBackup

Privacy policy

Updated 19 October 2026

ObsidianBackup is a personal-use backup utility operated by nick-konovalchuk for the owner's own Obsidian vault and Google Drive account.

Data and purpose

The utility uploads timestamped vault ZIP archives and Git bundles to the owner's private Google Drive. Archives can contain the owner's notes, configuration and committed history. File names, sizes and checksums are used to transfer and verify backups.

Google access is limited to the drive.file scope: files created by the app or explicitly selected for it. The utility uses this access to create, list, read and verify those backup files. OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored in the local rclone configuration on the owner's Mac.

Storage and sharing

Local archives remain on the owner's Mac; uploaded archives are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Archives are uploaded without additional archive encryption, by the owner's choice. Access depends on the owner's Drive sharing settings and account protections.

The utility has no separate hosted application backend, advertising or analytics. It does not publish vault content through this website.

Retention and control

Backups are append-only and are kept until the owner removes them. The owner can delete cloud archives in Google Drive and local archives on the Mac, revoke the app's access in Google Account settings, and remove saved credentials from the local rclone configuration. Revoking access does not delete existing archives or local copies.

This website

These static pages contain no forms, tracking scripts or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process access information under GitHub's privacy statement. Google Drive storage is subject to Google's privacy policy.
