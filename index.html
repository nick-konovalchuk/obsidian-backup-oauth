Personal backup utility

ObsidianBackup

Backups of a local Obsidian vault, stored in its owner's private Google Drive.

This is a personal-use utility operated by nick-konovalchuk. It uses rclone on the owner's Mac to upload timestamped ZIP archives and Git bundles.

Google Drive access

The utility requests the drive.file permission to create, read and verify backup files it creates or that the owner explicitly selects for it. Google OAuth credentials remain in the local rclone configuration.

This website describes the utility. Vault notes and backup archives are not published here.
